HOW TO PLAY

🖥 Desktop Controls

  • W A S D or Arrow Keys — Move Character
  • Mouse Cursor — Aim
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • R — Reload Weapon
  • 1 2 3 4 — Switch Weapon
  • Spacebar — Quick Dash
  • ESC or P — Pause Game

📱 Mobile Controls

  • Left Virtual Joystick — Drag to Move
  • Right Shoot Button — Hold or Tap to Fire
  • Reload / Dash Buttons — Quick Action Buttons

🧟 Gameplay Tips

  • Survive as many waves & nights as possible!
  • Night fall increases zombie speed and spawn rates. Flashlight visibility is vital.
  • Collect coins dropped by zombies or found in crate drops to purchase weapons and permanent stat upgrades.
  • Watch out for dangerous Tank and Boss Zombies!
